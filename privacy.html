Public trial

The public CARRR Payroll trial is intended for fictional or anonymised data only. Do not submit real employee, bank, tax, National Insurance, company payroll or other confidential information to a public deployment.

Real payroll use

Handling real payroll data would require an appropriate private deployment, defined data controller/processor responsibilities, security controls, retention rules, access management and privacy documentation suitable for the organisation and jurisdiction.

Website analytics and cookies

The static website package supplied here does not itself add analytics or marketing cookies. If analytics, embedded services, advertising or other tracking technologies are added later, the privacy and cookie information should be updated accordingly.
