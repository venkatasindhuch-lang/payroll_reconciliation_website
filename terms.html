CARRR PAYROLL

Terms information

Draft website text: obtain appropriate legal review before relying on these terms for a commercial service.

Purpose

CARRR Payroll is presented as a payroll checking, analysis, reconciliation and review workflow. It is not a payroll payment service and does not replace the employer's payroll system, statutory obligations or professional payroll judgement.

Public demonstration

The public demonstration should be used only with fictional or anonymised data. Users are responsible for ensuring that they have authority to upload any information to the environment they use.

Release decision

A CARRR "Ready to Release" result is a workflow/control outcome based on the configured checks and supplied data. It does not guarantee payroll accuracy, legal compliance, tax correctness, banking accuracy or successful payment processing.

Production use

Before production use, the software, hosting, controls, security, privacy, data retention, business continuity and legal requirements should be independently reviewed for the intended organisation and jurisdiction.
